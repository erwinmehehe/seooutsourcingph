# Editorial and evidence sign-off

For each page, assign a responsible reviewer before changing `noindex` or publishing the WordPress draft. The reviewer should inspect:

1. **First-hand evidence:** Does the page show approved work products, genuinely observed data, or a clearly labeled hypothetical example? Never turn templates into disguised client case studies.
2. **Buyer usefulness:** What can someone decide or do with the page that they could not after reading a generic SEO blog? Remove passages that do not change a decision.
3. **Company truthfulness:** Verify staff identities, location, operator name, partnerships, pricing, service capacity, certifications and testimonials. Do not infer them from branding or generated imagery.
4. **Technical accuracy:** Confirm implementation instructions against actual CMS/hosting and the current official platform documentation.
5. **Legal review:** Privacy/terms/cookies need responsible approval; Australian healthcare/dental treatment claims require appropriate specialist review. The current version remains a draft.
6. **Conversion testing:** A test lead must arrive in the configured system with audience, plan and service intact; give visitors an accurate acknowledgement and a working privacy link.
7. **SEO intent:** Confirm the page targets its assigned distinct query, links to related pages naturally, and does not compete with the EMD homepage.

**Do not mark `APPROVED` based solely on automated checks.**
