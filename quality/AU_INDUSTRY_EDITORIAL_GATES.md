# Australia industry content expansion — review gates

The Australia hub and ten new sector pages are **draft-only** and `noindex` in the standalone build and WordPress import. Seven existing industry pages include new Australia-specific guidance. No Australian office, Australian practitioner licence, SEO client, performance outcome or testimonial is claimed.

## Before indexing any page

1. Have the Australian business operator verify offered services, real service areas, hours, eligible customers, licences, authorisations and customer contact routes.
2. Have an appropriate regulated-sector reviewer approve medical, legal, tax, disability service, solar-energy or property-marketing claims where applicable. Australian legal requirements can differ by state/territory.
3. Replace hypothetical planning scenarios with authentic, permission-cleared work **only if** such evidence exists; otherwise label them as examples.
4. Verify linked official regulator sources are current, and confirm whether country targeting matches the business's actual delivery remit.
5. Perform a real WordPress import test and inspect H1s, mobile page layout, footer, breadcrumbs, structured data, form routing, image/asset paths, sitemap and indexing settings.
6. Publish **only substantively approved** pages. Do not launch all new pages at once. Consider releasing dental/home-services first where there is supporting business evidence.

## Geographic strategy

These pages serve **Australian customers of a Philippines-based SEO team**. Do not create GBP profiles or “SEO agency in Sydney/Melbourne/Brisbane” location landing pages suggesting a physical Australian office that does not exist.
