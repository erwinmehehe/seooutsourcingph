SEO support for your client accounts

Your agency may need help with a specific account, a backlog of technical fixes or regular content production. A white-label arrangement lets you assign that work without handing over the client relationship.

Send the websites, target markets and work you need covered. We quote for the agreed tasks and identify what requires your approval, developer support or client input before work begins.

What you can outsource

Keyword research and page planning

Research customer searches, review competing pages and map topics to existing or proposed URLs. The output identifies page intent and the gaps to address, rather than a list of keywords without a plan.

Technical and on-page SEO

Review crawling, indexing, internal links, page titles and content structure. Recommendations name the affected URLs and distinguish changes we can implement from work for your developer. Agreed implementation includes follow-up checks.

Content and local search

Request service-page briefs, article briefs, content updates or location-page recommendations. Business details and claims need client confirmation. Local search work can include Google Business Profile recommendations and checks for inconsistent business information.

Reporting

Receive a record of completed work, open issues and proposed next steps. Where access is provided, reporting can include Search Console and analytics data. Rankings are reported with their market and date; they are not a substitute for enquiries or sales data.

Your brand, your review process

Share the templates and task system your agency uses. Agree a contact person, review dates and who signs off changes. Client-facing files can use your branding; direct client communication happens only when requested.

Access requirements depend on the work. Use individual user accounts and grant only the permissions needed. Do not send passwords through the quote form.

Choose a project or ongoing support

A project suits a defined task such as a technical review or content plan. Monthly support suits a continuing queue of implementation, content and reporting work. The proposal specifies the tasks, review process and price for your accounts.

Questions agencies ask

Can you work on accounts outside the Philippines?

Yes. Share each client's target country, language and service area so the research and recommendations match that market.

Do you contact our clients?

Your agency remains the client contact unless you request a different arrangement. Client-facing communication and reporting are agreed in the scope.

Can we start with one account?

Yes. Send one website and the tasks you want covered. The quote will define the initial assignment and review process.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Search results depend on competition, website conditions and search-engine changes. We agree the work and report what changes without promising a ranking position.
