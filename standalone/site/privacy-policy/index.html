DRAFT POLICY — MUST BE REVIEWED BY THE SITE OWNER AND LEGAL COUNSEL. Production-review required

This is a privacy policy draft for the supplied website package. The site owner must verify actual hosting, email/CRM providers, analytics, data transfers, and retention before publication. Nothing here is a claim about the current live website's full practices.

Who is responsible for the website?

SEO Outsourcing Philippines is the site brand. For privacy requests and questions about an inquiry, contact info@seooutsourcingphilippines.com. Add verified business registration and controller information if legally required.

Information collected through contact forms

The supplied form asks for a name, email, company, website URL, agency or business selection, support plan, service interest, and project details. Do not include passwords, patient records, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Why the information is used

The server processes data to evaluate and respond to requested services, protect the form from abuse, and maintain necessary inquiry records. It does not automatically enroll the sender in a promotional email list. If marketing subscriptions are added, they need a separate lawful basis and controls.

Storage and retention in the supplied backend

The optional Python backend stores inquiries in a private SQLite database on the configured host. Its default retention is 90 days, configurable by the site owner. It uses a short-lived form session cookie and a hashed network identifier for abuse controls. The production operator must confirm backups, deletion, and incident handling.

Sharing and third parties

Submitted data may be delivered to an authorized company email account if SMTP notifications are configured. Your hosting and email providers may process data under their own agreements. The supplied static pages do not include advertising pixels or third-party analytics by default. Any later CRM, analytics, chat tool, or advertising integration must be documented here before launch.

Security and individual requests

Use HTTPS, restricted admin access, server updates, and appropriate access controls. Contact the email above to request access, correction, or deletion as applicable. Requests may need identity verification and can be subject to lawful retention requirements.

Cookies and future changes

See the Cookie Policy for the basic form session behavior. Update this document whenever hosting, data practices, or integrated services change. A qualified legal reviewer should check it against applicable data-protection requirements.
