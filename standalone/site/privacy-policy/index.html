Privacy policy

How we handle website enquiries.

Information you provide

When you request a quote, we ask for your name, email address, website and details of the work you need. You may also provide your company name and target market. Please do not submit passwords, patient information or confidential client records.

How we use your information

We use enquiry details to respond, prepare a quote and discuss services you request. A quote request does not subscribe you to marketing. Technical request information may be used to protect the form against abuse.

Service providers

Hosting and email providers process information needed to run the website and deliver enquiries. If you choose to book through Calendly, its privacy policy applies to information you provide there.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits and how visitors use the website. Our configured events record discovery-call link clicks and quote-confirmation page views. These events do not include the name, email address or message entered in the quote form.

Google processes website usage and device information for this service and may use analytics cookies. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and our cookie policy.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information for the time needed to respond and manage any resulting business relationship, subject to applicable record-keeping requirements. Access is limited to people who need the information for those purposes.

Your requests

Contact info@seooutsourcingphilippines.com to ask about access, correction or deletion of your enquiry information, or to withdraw consent for further contact. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Changes

We update this page when the way we handle website information changes. See our cookie policy for information about browser storage.
