How we work

Agree the work, review the delivery and decide what to do next.

Get a QuoteView sample deliverables

1. Tell us what you need

Share your website, target market and priorities. Let us know which tasks your team already handles and where you need support. You do not need to send account passwords with your enquiry.

2. Review the proposal

Your proposal sets out the tasks, deliverables, fees and delivery dates. It identifies who will implement changes, what access is needed and how reviews and revisions will work.

3. Provide access and project information

Once the work is agreed, provide the relevant website or reporting access and business information. We confirm outstanding questions before starting tasks that depend on those answers.

4. Review the delivery

Review the recommendations, drafts or completed changes against the agreed scope. Work that still needs your approval or developer support is identified separately from changes already made.

5. Plan the next steps

For ongoing work, reports cover completed tasks, changes in search performance and recommended priorities. The reporting format and review schedule are agreed before work starts.

For agency clients

Your agency remains the client contact unless you request a different arrangement. We use the templates and review process agreed for the account.
