The minimum information to send for a useful proposal

Start with the client website, the Australian market it serves, its current platform, the specific symptom or backlog, and the person who can approve changes. If the client uses WordPress, Shopify, an internal development team or an outside legal reviewer, say so. Those dependencies determine what can be delivered.

  • Website URL and a few commercially important pages.
  • Agency delivery model and whether direct client contact is prohibited.
  • Existing audit findings, ticket format or publishing workflow.
  • Approved market facts, business-service areas and known compliance requirements.
  • Requested first handoff and what the reviewer will accept as complete.

What “programmatic” means on this site

The pages come from a structured, version-controlled content model. The model checks for an original use case, a page-specific worked example, independent reference material, a usable worksheet and an acceptance test. It does not automatically generate every industry-by-city combination. New pages stay out of indexing until an editor checks search intent and real buyer usefulness.

Where two queries ask for the same solution, we prefer one strong page and a relevant internal link. This avoids creating a large collection of look-alike landing pages whose only role is to send visitors to Contact.

How an Australian agency can assess a Philippines-based partner

Request a narrowly scoped pilot before moving an entire client roster. Evaluate whether the team asked for the right facts, documented exceptions, respected publication access, and delivered evidence that another specialist can reproduce. Keep client ownership, pricing, and sign-off rules explicit. Meet the operating brand, examine sample issue records, and read the handoff method.

Do not rely on unverified ranking claims or anonymous case-study graphics. The Australian dental tracking examples on this website are dated observations and are not evidence that a particular workstream produced revenue.

What should not go into an SEO outsourcing proposal

Exclude invented Australian office locations, guaranteed ranking packages, bulk doorway suburb pages and client case studies without permission. Also separate technical recommendations from implementation: a finished audit PDF is not proof that code or CMS content changed. The agreement should say who can modify live assets and who will test the outcome.
