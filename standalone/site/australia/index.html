The minimum information to send for a useful proposal

Start with the client website, the Australian market it serves, its current platform, the specific symptom or backlog, and the person who can approve changes. If the client uses WordPress, Shopify, an internal development team or an outside legal reviewer, say so. Those dependencies determine what can be delivered.

  • Website URL and a few commercially important pages.
  • Agency delivery model and whether direct client contact is prohibited.
  • Existing audit findings, ticket format or publishing workflow.
  • Approved market facts, business-service areas and known compliance requirements.
  • The first deliverable, deadline and review contact.

How an Australian agency can assess a Philippines-based partner
