SEO reporting

Reports covering completed work, search performance and the next priorities for your website or client accounts.

What your report can include

  • Completed tasks and the pages affected
  • Search Console clicks, impressions and query trends
  • Tracked keyword positions with dates and search settings
  • Analytics and enquiries, where tracking is available
  • Outstanding work, questions and next priorities

We agree the metrics and reporting period before preparing the report. Comparisons use consistent date ranges and filters, with tracking changes and missing data explained.

Keep the conclusions tied to the data

A ranking increase does not, by itself, show that sales or enquiries improved. Reports distinguish observed changes from the work completed and explain where the available data cannot establish a connection.

Use your agency’s format

White-label reports can use your branding, terminology and template. Your agency reviews the report and manages client communication. We agree the delivery schedule and review process as part of the scope.
