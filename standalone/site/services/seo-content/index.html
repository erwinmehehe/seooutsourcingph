Content starts with buyer questions

SEO content outsourcing is most effective when the assignment includes a real audience, an answerable question, supporting evidence, and a clear page purpose. A list of keywords is a starting signal, not a complete editorial brief.

Unreviewed content creates risk: duplicated topics, unsupported promises, incorrect service details, and pages that compete with each other. A documented brief keeps research, subject-matter review, drafting, revisions, and publication connected.

Source-backed briefs and reviews

We establish a content map before drafting. Each brief records the target reader, search intent, primary page, related questions, sources, internal links, required proof, voice rules, and CTA. Drafts pass editorial and on-page QA before handoff. Specialist topics require subject-matter approval.

The standard for completion

Accept content when its claims are sourced, the angle differs from existing pages, internal links are useful and an authorized reviewer has approved publishing.

What editors receive and approve

  • Search-intent research and editorial topic maps
  • Content briefs with sources, structure, and link targets
  • Service pages, educational guides, and content refreshes
  • Editorial QA, duplication checks, and revision log
  • Publishing handoff with metadata and internal links

The seo content handoff in practice

  1. Prioritize content gaps and existing-page refresh opportunities
  2. Approve the brief and business claims
  3. Write and edit against the approved structure
  4. Review, publish, link, and monitor the page

What we need from you

  • Brand voice examples and prohibited language
  • Product/service facts and qualified reviewers
  • Access to existing content and query data
  • Publishing calendar and approval owner

How to evaluate progress

Track accepted briefs, substantive revisions, published pages and query coverage. Do not treat a word-count target as proof of editorial quality.

  • Approved content produced and published
  • Priority topics covered without overlap
  • Organic landing-page engagement
  • Qualified actions related to content paths

Content quality is decided before drafting

A writer cannot invent the client’s price, clinical advice, warranty or selling proposition. Give them approved source notes and state which claims need review. A strong brief identifies what the page will not claim as well as what it should explain.

StageWhat to check
ResearchDocument the reader’s decision, competing page intent and approved source links.
DraftCover the useful questions with accurate evidence and planned internal links.
ReviewReject unsupported claims, duplicated sections and generic endings; record the authorized editor.

Questions clients ask

Can the writing be white-label?

Content can be delivered unbranded under your editorial rules when the arrangement is agreed.

Do you write regulated medical or financial content?

We can support research and drafting, but factual, clinical, financial or legal claims need review by an appropriately qualified client-side subject-matter expert.

Should we publish as many pages as possible?

No. We prioritize distinct topics and refreshing strong existing URLs over producing numerous near-duplicates.
