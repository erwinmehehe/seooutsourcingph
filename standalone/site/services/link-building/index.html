Research opportunities without shortcuts

Authority work should support useful content and real relationships. The objective is not a spreadsheet filled with links of unknown value. It is a careful process for identifying relevant opportunities, reviewing placements, and recording what was actually earned or approved.

Low-quality placements can waste money and create search risk. Outsourced link support needs explicit editorial standards, exclusion rules, human review, and evidence of the live destination. Price and quantity alone are poor proxies for quality.

Prospecting, risk checks and permissions

We start with the target pages and the types of websites that serve the same audience. Prospects are evaluated for topical relevance, editorial fit, and obvious warning signals. Outreach copy and potential placements go through the agreed approval process. Reporting distinguishes prospecting, outreach, accepted placements, and live verification.

The standard for completion

Verify the live linking page, editorial context, actual destination, link attributes and placement date. Reject undisclosed paid links that violate applicable platform rules.

Authority research outputs and evidence

  • Topically relevant prospect lists with review notes
  • Outreach briefs and editorial placement guidelines
  • Anchor and destination review to avoid over-optimization
  • Live URL and link-attribute verification where applicable
  • Status reports distinguishing opportunities from completed work

The link building handoff in practice

  1. Define audiences, landing pages, and quality standards
  2. Research and qualify prospects
  3. Secure approvals and coordinate outreach as scoped
  4. Verify live details and record exceptions

What we need from you

  • Approved target pages and brand policies
  • Restricted topics, partners, and competitor exclusions
  • Approver for outreach or sponsorship decisions
  • Disclosure requirements where any placement is sponsored

How to evaluate progress

Report approved prospects, earned or authorized placements, rejected opportunities and live-link QA. Link counts are not a measure of ranking improvement.

  • Qualified prospects reviewed
  • Editorial outreach and response quality
  • Approved live links with correct destinations
  • Referral traffic and relevance where measurable

Questions clients ask

Do you guarantee a number of links or rankings?

Any deliverable commitment must be defined in a proposal. Search rankings and third-party editorial decisions are not guaranteed.

Do you buy placements?

Paid or sponsored placements must follow applicable search guidance and disclosures. We do not present paid links as organic editorial endorsements.

Can you work with our existing outreach process?

Yes, prospecting, approvals, templates, and QA can be mapped to the agency workflow.
