Turn research into a usable roadmap

Keyword research is valuable only if it affects what the team does next. For an established site, the work often involves consolidating overlapping pages, identifying under-served buying questions, and selecting a few services with the best evidence-to-effort ratio.

Search Console queries, rank tracking, competitor pages, and commercial priorities show different parts of the opportunity. A high-impression term does not automatically require its own URL. And a low-volume term may still describe a high-value buyer.

Choose priorities and dependencies

We group queries by intent and stage, identify the strongest existing destination, document new page gaps, and determine which assets or links are needed. Priorities account for current visibility, content quality, business fit, competition, and available implementation capacity.

The standard for completion

A strategy is ready when the team can identify which URL serves each intent, who owns each task and what evidence will decide the next priority.

Strategy outputs a team can implement

  • Intent-clustered keyword and query map
  • Page ownership and cannibalization plan
  • Competitor and content gap assessment
  • Quarterly roadmap with dependencies and reviewers
  • Measurement plan for priority pages and conversions

The seo strategy handoff in practice

  1. Review business goals and search evidence
  2. Group related queries and assess existing URLs
  3. Prioritize updates, consolidations, and new pages
  4. Approve and hand off the work queue

What we need from you

  • Revenue-driving services and target markets
  • GSC, analytics, and ranking exports
  • Current sitemap and conversion definitions
  • Approval for business priority tradeoffs

How to evaluate progress

Measure roadmap execution, addressed high-value search intents, accepted briefs and business outcomes where reliably attributed.

  • Priority intents mapped to a strong URL
  • Approved optimization roadmap
  • Non-branded query and page coverage
  • Relevant conversions by target area

Keyword-to-URL prioritization example

Suppose Search Console shows impressions for “outsource SEO Philippines,” “SEO outsourcing Philippines,” and “outsource SEO to the Philippines.” The page intent overlaps.

Impressions are not keyword search volume. Use page-level Search Console performance and qualified lead data when prioritizing future work.

An SEO roadmap needs a “not now” column

A keyword spreadsheet is not a strategy unless it forces trade-offs. A sensible roadmap may defer new blog topics until service pages have clear intent, useful content, links and measurement.

StageWhat to check
NowFix indexing and the highest-intent pages that already have evidence of demand.
NextBuild missing supporting content once the core pages and internal link relationships are sound.
LaterAvoid speculative expansion pages or unsupported industries until a business owner can provide real information.

Questions clients ask

Should every search query become a page?

No. Closely related variants typically share a page. Unique pages should answer materially different questions.

Can you work from our Search Console export?

Yes. Impressions and clicks help uncover demand signals, but they need context from page performance and buyer intent.

Do you support international targeting?

International research can be included with separate language, market, and technical requirements.
