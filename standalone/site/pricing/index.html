What a good proposal specifies

A price should come with the website scope, workstream, implementation responsibilities, review cadence, tool access, dependencies, and exclusions. A higher plan may offer broader coordinated execution, but that does not promise a fixed number of articles, links, or hours unless specified in writing.

Before choosing a retainer

A starting rate is not a workload specification.

These questions often change the real cost of outsourced SEO more than the headline monthly price.

Ask the providerWhy it affects scopeWritten answer to request
