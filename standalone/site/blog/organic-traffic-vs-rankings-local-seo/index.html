Organic Traffic vs Rankings: What Local Businesses Should Care About

A ranking is one observation

A keyword position describes where a page or profile appeared for a particular search. It does not tell you how many people clicked, whether they wanted that service or whether they became customers. Keep the keyword, date, location, device and tracking method with each observation.

Follow the visit to the page

Use Search Console to review the queries and pages receiving clicks. Separate business-name searches from searches for services. In analytics, check what those visitors did next, where tracking is available. A gain in visits to an information article can mean something different from a gain on a booking page.

Investigate conflicting signals

If positions rise but enquiries fall, check the affected pages, enquiry forms, call handling and tracking before drawing a conclusion. If traffic rises but the enquiries are irrelevant, review the search terms and service information. Report these findings separately rather than combining them into a single success score.
