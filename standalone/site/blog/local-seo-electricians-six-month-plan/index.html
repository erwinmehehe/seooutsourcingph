Local SEO for Electricians: A Six-Month Ranking Plan

Choose the services to promote

Separate residential repairs, installations, inspections and commercial work where the business provides them. Confirm the actual coverage, operating hours and any qualifications the business wants to publish. Those details belong in the brief before service pages are written.

Sequence the website work

Review existing pages and business-profile information first. Identify missing service details, inconsistent contact information and technical problems affecting important pages. Agree which changes can be published directly and which need the business or developer to review them.

Use the six-month review to assess delivery and demand

Report completed changes separately from search positions and enquiries. Compare the same services and tracking settings over time, and note changes in call handling or availability. Review whether calls match the jobs the business wants. The plan provides a work schedule; it does not guarantee positions by a particular month.
