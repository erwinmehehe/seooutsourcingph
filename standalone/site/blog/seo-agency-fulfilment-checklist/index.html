The SEO Agency Fulfilment Checklist: From Sale to Monthly Report

Confirm what was sold

Carry the agreed services, website coverage, fees, dates and exclusions from the proposal into the delivery brief. Identify which commitments depend on the client or developer. Do this before assigning production tasks.

Arrange access and ownership

Record the website and reporting accounts required, the person who can grant access and the contact who approves work. Use the agency’s agreed task system and file structure. Client information should remain separated by account.

Close the reporting cycle

Check which tasks were completed, which were published and what remains blocked. Link the report to the affected pages or deliverables and identify next actions. Before adding work for the next month, account for outstanding tasks and the agency’s review capacity.
