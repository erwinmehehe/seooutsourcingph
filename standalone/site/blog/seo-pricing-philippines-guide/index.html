SEO Pricing in the Philippines: What Buyers Should Compare

Why starting prices are only one part of an SEO outsourcing decision.

Practical guide · Evaluation criteria, examples and next steps

Compare a quote by its deliverables, approval requirements and costs outside the retainer. A headline monthly price does not tell you how much execution capacity is included.

Different service models create different prices

An audit, a recurring execution lane, and dedicated specialists have different cost structures. Comparing them by headline price alone can mislead buyers. The proposal should define what the team will do, which tools are needed, who approves work, and what falls outside the agreement.

  • One-time scope versus ongoing queue
  • Specialist mix and implementation requirements
  • Reporting and communication cadence

What should appear in a proposal

Ask for an explicit service list, first-cycle priorities, access requirements, deliverable acceptance rules, reporting schedule, third-party cost treatment, and a process for scope changes. Vague phrases such as 'full SEO' or 'unlimited optimization' are not enough to estimate the work.

  • Deliverables and boundaries
  • Implementation and publishing ownership
  • Review milestones and dependencies

Consider the hidden costs

Internal coordination, developer time, design, licensed tools, paid placements, and subject-matter review may all be outside the vendor fee. Include these in the total plan so an apparently inexpensive package does not surprise the team later.

  • Internal account management
  • Developer and content approvals
  • External subscriptions and placements

Use a pilot if the relationship is new

A paid, representative first project can show whether the team understands the website, delivers usable evidence, and handles revisions promptly. It is often more informative than negotiating a long contract based only on a sample sales presentation.

  • One accepted deliverable
  • Review time and quality
  • Communication and escalation process

How to read published starting rates

SEO Outsourcing Philippines currently publishes starting monthly prices for Essential, Growth, and Scale on its Pricing page. They are entry points, not automatic commitments to a fixed number of articles, links, or hours. Actual scope must be confirmed for the site and market involved.

  • Compare the published plans
  • Ask what volume fits the agreed scope
  • Approve changes before increasing workload

Compare pricing models before comparing headline rates

The website currently advertises SEO Essential from $700, SEO Growth from $1,000 and SEO Scale from $1,500 per month. Those are advertised starting points, not fixed output quantities or a universal rate for every website.

Compare written scope, not currency alone

A low monthly quote can exclude development, stakeholder reviews, content approval, tools and client reporting. A higher quote can also be poor value if outputs and acceptance are undefined. Compare proposals line by line before deciding.

  1. One-time onboarding and audit cost separated from recurring delivery.
  2. Pages, sites and profiles included or explicitly excluded.
  3. Implementation owner and the number of revision cycles or change procedure.
  4. Reporting source, frequency and sign-off responsibility.

Frequently asked questions

Why do some SEO plans cost more than others?

Specialization, website complexity, capacity, implementation, content, and reporting requirements can change the scope.

Does a low price mean poor SEO?

Not automatically. Inspect accepted work, management effort, and genuine evidence rather than infer quality solely from price.

Are rankings guaranteed in a monthly package?

No. The agreement can define deliverables, but search outcomes depend on factors outside a provider's control.

The real price is more than the retainer

An illustration: a $1,000 monthly service fee plus five internal review hours at an assumed $60 per hour totals $1,300 of monthly contribution cost before software, revisions and internal management overhead. Those inputs are examples, not the agency’s quoted staffing costs.
