SEO Link Building Outsourcing: How to Protect Quality and Brand Safety

Agree the prospect criteria first

Identify the pages you want to support and the audiences they serve. Review prospective websites for relevant content, a plausible readership and an editorial reason to mention your business. A third-party authority score alone does not establish that a placement is useful.

Keep costs and placement decisions visible

Agree who approves websites, outreach copy and any fees before commitments are made. The report should distinguish research, outreach, accepted proposals and live links. Ask how rejected prospects, substitutions and links removed after publication are handled.

Inspect the published link

Check the destination URL, surrounding text, link attributes and whether the page is accessible. Record the publication date and any agreed conditions. Third-party editorial decisions and search rankings are outside an SEO provider’s control; evaluate the actual placement rather than a promised ranking increase.
