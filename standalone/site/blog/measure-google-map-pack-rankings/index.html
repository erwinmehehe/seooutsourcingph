Map-Pack Rankings: How to Measure Google Maps Visibility Properly

The central challenge is avoiding misleading conclusions from a single search performed near the business address. Consider a dentist measuring visibility across a 7-by-7 grid around an Australian or US suburb.

Measure visibility across a defined geographic grid, device, keyword set, and time period.

Key takeaways
  • Measure visibility across a defined geographic grid, device, keyword set, and time period.
  • Prioritise map grids, search origin, proximity, categories before expanding the scope or production volume.
  • Track Top-Three Grid Points, Average Map Position, Share Of Local Voice with consistent definitions and data sources.
  • Record approvals, dependencies and implementation evidence so delays and ownership stay visible.

Primary-source note: Google Business Profile local ranking guidance. Google explains that local results are mainly shaped by relevance, distance and prominence, and recommends complete, accurate business information.

How to apply the framework in practice

Use the example of a dentist measuring visibility across a 7-by-7 grid around an Australian or US suburb as a test. The plan should explain what happens first, what can wait, what the business must supply, how quality is approved and which signal would justify expanding the scope. If those answers are missing, more content, more keywords or more reports will usually add management load before they add value.

How to measure progress without vanity reporting

For Map-Pack Rankings: How to Measure Google Maps Visibility Properly, separate implementation signals from business outcomes. Keep the data source, filters and date range consistent so changes can be compared fairly.

  • Top-Three Grid Points: Define the source, owner, date range and decision this measure supports. Keep the method stable, annotate major changes and avoid reporting the number without an interpretation or next action.
  • Average Map Position: Use the same keyword, business, device, search origin and tool each time. Report distribution and coverage, not only the best point, and connect movement to the page or profile changes made during the period.
  • Share Of Local Voice: Define the source, owner, date range and decision this measure supports. Keep the method stable, annotate major changes and avoid reporting the number without an interpretation or next action.
  • Profile Calls: Count the action by source, landing page, service and location, then review whether it was genuine, in scope and commercially useful. Raw volume should not include spam, existing-customer support or wrong-service enquiries.
  • Coverage By Service: Define the source, owner, date range and decision this measure supports. Keep the method stable, annotate major changes and avoid reporting the number without an interpretation or next action.
