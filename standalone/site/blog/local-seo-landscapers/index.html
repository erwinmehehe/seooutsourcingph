Local SEO for Landscapers: From Map Rankings to Quote Requests

Separate construction from maintenance

Garden design, paving, planting and recurring maintenance attract different enquiries. Explain what each service includes, the area covered and any project requirements. Avoid making a maintenance page carry the details of a large design-and-build service.

Use projects to answer practical questions

Show the starting conditions, work completed and materials or planting choices where the business can supply that information. Include original photographs with permission. Explain constraints such as site access or maintenance requirements when relevant to the customer’s decision.

Check the enquiry quality

Review requests by job type, location and whether the team can quote for them. Keep business-profile details and service pages consistent with current coverage. When visits increase without suitable enquiries, inspect the searches, page content and contact route rather than assuming more traffic is the answer.
