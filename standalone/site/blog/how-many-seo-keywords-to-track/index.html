How Many SEO Keywords Should You Track?

Choose distinct services and locations to track, with a relevant page for each search.

Practical guide · Evaluation criteria, examples and next steps

Track enough queries to explain decisions at the URL and intent level. Adding hundreds of near-identical variants rarely improves a monthly discussion.

Start with business decisions

The right keyword set should show whether the campaign is reaching the correct audience and pages. An independent local practice and a multi-category online retailer need different tracking models. More tracked phrases are not automatically more informative.

  • Priority services and buyer intents
  • Actual market and device conditions
  • Landing pages tied to action

Group by intent, not tiny variations

A phrase like 'outsource SEO Philippines' and 'outsource SEO to the Philippines' may belong to one commercial intent cluster. Tracking both can help understand phrasing, but building separate pages for each is usually unnecessary. Group close variants and choose representative terms for trend monitoring.

  • One primary intent per strong URL
  • Representative phrase variants
  • Avoid double-counting closely related terms

Choose a tiered keyword set

A useful model separates core money queries, supporting service queries, informational queries, and competitor context. Review the full tracked set periodically so irrelevant phrases do not obscure performance. Search Console can provide broader discovery, while a rank tracker offers fixed-location measurements.

  • Core commercial positions
  • Supporting topical coverage
  • Long-tail and research signals

Keep measurement conditions stable

Positions vary by location, device, personalization, and date. Record the chosen conditions and avoid comparing one city's desktop positions with another market's mobile data. Track visibility distribution and important landing pages rather than only an average position.

  • Location and device
  • Search engine and tracking cadence
  • Fixed sources and definitions

Connect rankings to meaningful actions

A keyword can improve while a page still fails to generate enquiries. Review conversion paths, branded versus non-branded traffic, and the fit between the searcher's task and the page. Use the findings to decide whether to improve content, technical behavior, or the offer.

  • Qualified leads
  • Page engagement
  • Conversion quality and attribution limits

Example tracking portfolio by decision purpose

Do not choose a keyword count before defining the questions your tracker must answer. A small local service business may need separate service and neighborhood groups, while a national e-commerce operation needs categories and template coverage.

Example or checkpointRecommended actionReview or acceptance
Core commercialTrack one representative set per product/service and locationCan target pages be found for their intended purchase queries?
Informational and comparisonTrack topic groups relevant to helpful guidesAre guides reaching the intended research audience?
Brand and non-brandSplit the groups for interpretationIs reported movement driven by name recognition or new discovery?

Keep the portfolio stable enough to compare periods, and document every replacement. Tracking 200 low-value variants is less useful than 35 queries selected to answer a specific commercial question.

Track the choices, not every spelling

A portfolio of 25 keywords can be more useful than 250 if each group represents a commercial decision. For a local service company, group emergency, planned service and brand queries separately; then review landing-page actions alongside tracked positions.

  1. Label each tracked term with the intended landing page and market.
  2. Note which keywords are branded, local, informational and commercial.
  3. Use a fixed location and device so trend comparisons are meaningful.
  4. Add or retire terms when they no longer support a decision.

Frequently asked questions

Is tracking 100 keywords better than 20?

Only if the larger set covers useful additional intents and can be reviewed meaningfully.

Should we track every Google Search Console query?

Use GSC for discovery and broader trends; choose a focused rank-tracking set for stable monitoring.

Are keywords in top ten always valuable?

No. Commercial value depends on intent, market, and what visitors do after landing.
