Help buyers find the right products

E-commerce sites are dynamic. Products change, filters create URL combinations, and merchandising priorities shift faster than a typical content calendar. Search visibility depends on category structure, indexation decisions, product information, internal links, and an honest measurement of revenue-related actions.

Catalog and technical problems worth prioritizing

Uncontrolled faceted navigation can create large crawl spaces. Thin product variants can compete with primary pages. Category pages may target broad terms without useful selection guidance. A generic SEO checklist will not resolve these issues without understanding the platform and how inventory changes.

Category, product and technical updates

Prioritize search demand against margin, stock, and category importance. Review crawl/indexation patterns, canonical behavior, navigation paths, product structured data, and the distinction between collections and editorial buying guides. Changes require coordination with merchandising and development.

Which catalog URLs should search engines actually index?

A practical deliverable is a category opportunity brief naming the primary collection, supporting filters, indexation recommendation, internal-link sources, required copy, and validation checks. A technical issue log should flag affected templates rather than only individual URLs.

Retail SEO questions before creating more category pages

Do you support Shopify or WooCommerce?

Platform-specific implementation depends on access, theme setup, and agreed developer scope. Audits and recommendations can be tailored to common commerce platforms.

Should every filter combination be indexed?

No. Indexation decisions should reflect distinct useful demand and avoid unnecessary duplicate combinations.

Do you work with inventory changes?

Content and technical rules can account for discontinued and seasonal products, including redirect or replacement decisions.

Plan around real business operations

Discuss your industry

Product information, current pricing, and stock availability should be confirmed against the live catalog and reviewed by the merchant.

Product availability, prices and categories

For an Australian retailer, a “linen bed sheets” category and a “queen fitted linen sheet” product page deserve different jobs. The page architecture should follow real product availability and catalogue depth, not duplicate every brand, colour and size combination.

Review current AUD prices, GST presentation where applicable, shipping regions, product availability and returns information using the merchant’s live catalogue. The ACCC warns against misleading price and availability claims, including where omitted details would change a buyer’s decision.

DecisionWhat to verify with the Australian business
Canonical logicKeep filter URLs and product variants under a controlled indexation plan
Product truthMatch offers, stock, delivery and prices to actual feed data
Shopping journeyTrack add-to-cart, checkout and qualified sales separately from impressions

Source for the relevant market rule: ACCC: false or misleading claims
