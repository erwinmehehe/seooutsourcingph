Coordinate branches without duplicating information

A multi-location business needs a structure that makes each real branch discoverable without turning the website into a stack of near-identical location pages. Search, operations, and customer support teams must agree on the source of truth for every location.

Where local data breaks at scale

Hours, staff, phone numbers, services, and closing status change. If web pages, GBP entries, directories, and internal location data do not stay aligned, customers encounter conflicting information and search engines receive mixed signals.

A governed multi-location SEO program

Build one location register, define page templates with mandatory unique details, map local search intent, and create an update/approval process. Crawl and structured-data checks should be performed at the template level, with spot checks at individual branches.

One practical multi location planning decision

A useful location page can include verified contact options, hours, services actually offered there, accessibility information when available, directions, and links to related service pages. A service-area business may require a different layout and should not claim storefronts.

Priority checks and outputs

  • Single verified location registry
  • Distinct branch content and accurate hours
  • Profile and directory ownership governance
  • Internal location hierarchy and local links
  • Calls, directions, and qualified conversions by branch

Australia market deep dive

Australian multi-location SEO requires a branch register before expansion

A business with offices in Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane needs each location’s address, service roster, appointment route and responsible manager kept current. City pages built from one template are not a substitute for real branch details.

Document actual opening/closure changes, GBP ownership, phone forwarding and site redirects in one register. Google’s rules distinguish real locations from virtual offices. A closed location should not continue appearing as an active office simply to preserve local visibility.

DecisionWhat to verify with the Australian business
Location masterVerified address, staff, services, hours and contact for each location
Change controlRecorded ownership for openings, closures, URL redirects and GBP amendments
ReportingBranch-specific outcomes rather than one misleading aggregate traffic figure
