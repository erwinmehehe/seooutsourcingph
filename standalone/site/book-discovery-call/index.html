What to expect

A practical conversation, not a generic sales pitch.

A discovery call is a chance to understand the work and clarify what an outsourcing arrangement would involve. We will not promise rankings, traffic, or revenue.

  • Your goals and constraints: your website, target markets, current priorities, and what you want to improve.
  • Potential SEO workstreams: technical SEO, local SEO, content, on-page optimization, reporting, or white-label agency support.
  • Next steps: what information is needed to prepare a realistic scope, deliverables, review process, and proposal.

What should I prepare?

Your website URL, your main SEO challenges, the type of support you are looking for, and the markets you serve are enough for an initial discussion. If you run an agency, mention whether you need white-label delivery and who approves client-site changes.

Common questions

Before you request a call

Is the first call a commitment?

No. It is an initial conversation to understand your needs. Any project requires a separate written scope and approval.

Can an agency ask about white-label support?

Yes. Tell us the services, expected volume, review process, and confidentiality requirements you need to discuss.

Will you review our site before the call?

Share the website and your priorities in the request. We can clarify what is feasible to review during an initial discussion and what requires a separate audit.
