03 / Choose your working model

Keep control of the decisions. Delegate the execution.

The handoff should look different for an agency managing clients and a business managing its own website.

For SEO and marketing agencies

Protect the client relationship.

Your team owns proposals, client communication and final approvals. A delivery partner can take a defined production queue without becoming your account manager.

Keep in-house
Client promises, brand standards, access decisions, publishing sign-off.
Delegate
Content briefs, technical tickets, on-page changes and unbranded reports.
First useful test
One accepted SEO ticket and one client-ready handoff through your own task board.
Plan an agency pilot →
For businesses and in-house marketers

Know what changed on your site.

Keep business facts, budget decisions and approvals close to the people who know the customers. Outsource diagnosis and agreed implementation work.

Keep in-house
Service facts, regulatory review, marketing goals and CMS ownership.
Delegate
Page improvements, crawl fixes, local SEO checks and outcome reporting.
First useful test
A review of one commercially important page with written fixes and a retest method.
Plan a business workstream →
