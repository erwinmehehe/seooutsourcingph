Essential contact form session

When the Python form backend is enabled, it issues a short-lived HTTP-only cookie named seo_sid. This associates a form session with a signed anti-forgery token. It is used to protect inquiry submission and is not an advertising identifier.

Analytics and advertising cookies

The packaged static website does not load third-party analytics or advertising pixels by default. If you later add GA4, ad tags, chat software, or other services, update this policy and implement any consent controls required in your markets.

Managing cookies

You can use your browser's controls to inspect or delete cookies. Disabling essential cookies can stop the protected contact form from submitting. You can still contact the team by email.

As the site moves from prototype to production, review the actual cookies and vendors. Contact info@seooutsourcingphilippines.com with questions.
