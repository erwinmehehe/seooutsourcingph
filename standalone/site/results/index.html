How to evaluate a ranking screenshot

Four questions to ask before you call it a case study

  1. Which keywords? A top-five count is less informative without the query set, locations, devices and search intent.
  2. Which time period? A dated snapshot is not a before-and-after comparison. Ask for a comparable baseline if improvement is claimed.
  3. What work was delivered? Request approved tickets or redacted audit findings to distinguish team output from outcomes driven by other factors.
  4. What happened after the click? If business performance is discussed, examine landing-page actions and qualified enquiries separately from ranking data.

The published dashboard links are retained for inspection; the website does not claim that their keyword counts establish revenue, causal impact or exclusive agency ownership.
