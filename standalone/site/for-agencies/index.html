The problem agencies need solved

Winning another account should not force an agency to improvise its delivery process. But adding disconnected freelancers can increase briefing, QA, and handoff work. A white-label partner is useful when the agency can provide clear priorities and receive work in a predictable format.

Who owns what

Your agency retains the commercial relationship, proposals to clients, final brand representation, and decisions about what can be promised or published. The delivery team handles only the assigned technical, content, local or reporting tasks. The reviewer checks acceptance criteria and raises exceptions before anything becomes client-ready.

A practical first engagement

Start with one representative account, or a workstream such as a technical issue audit and prioritized fixes. Supply a brief, source tools, review owner and time window. Track completed tasks, revisions, access gaps, and the time your staff spent supervising. Expand only when the handoff is reliable.

What to request in the proposal

  • Scope and planned workstreams
  • Named role responsible for review and escalation
  • Agency/client communication boundaries
  • Delivery files and reporting templates
  • Revision, change control, and confidentiality requirements

Common questions

Can all deliverables be white-label?

Unbranded documents can be included with approved formats and confidentiality terms.

Will you manage multiple client accounts?

Capacity and the number of accounts must be defined in a proposal after reviewing complexity and workload.

Can we use our own project tools?

Workflow alignment can be included, subject to access, privacy and operating constraints.

Illustrative client-account handoff

A four-part handoff keeps the account manager in control of the client relationship.

Buyer or scenarioRecommended pathReview standard
Agency leadAssign target URLs, constraints and client deliverable formatApproved client-facing scope
SEO delivery ownerComplete research, drafts or technical ticketsEvidence and source files linked in the agency system
QA reviewerCheck claims, URLs, source dates and acceptance criteriaReturn or approve with documented reasons
Agency account managerPresent accepted work and request client approvalClient retains publishing and commercial authority

A white-label arrangement needs a documented protocol for access, deadlines and escalation. It should not grant direct client contact by default.

Agency pilot

A low-risk pilot for an agency account

A pilot should exercise the real review workflow before multiple clients are delegated.

Illustrative scope: pick one service page from one client, supply brand/offer facts and the current target queries, and request an optimization brief rather than an entire campaign. The outsourcing partner prepares recommendations; your strategist decides what ships.

StageWhat should be inspectable
Agency inputService facts, client restrictions, existing page, target intent
Delivery outputAnnotated brief with proposed title, headings, internal links, evidence and approval needs
Reviewer checkNo unsupported claims; right URL; working links; sufficient source facts
DecisionApprove, revise or reject; record feedback before expanding the workstream

For multi-client workflows, request separate folders, credentials, task owners and approval rules for every account. A cheaper delivery quote is not economical if revisions consume your strategist’s time.
